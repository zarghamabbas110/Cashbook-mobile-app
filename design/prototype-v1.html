What to look at

  • The carry-forward strip on the home card — opening, in, out, closing. That replaces creating a book per month.
  • The Household / Personal split on every entry, and how Reports rolls it up across all four people.
  • The Transfer entry in the ledger — money moving between your own accounts, not counted as spending.
