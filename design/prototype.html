What changed in v2

  • Motion. The + opens a sheet that slides up over the app; tapping an account pushes the ledger in from the right. Lists settle in behind each screen.
  • Dates unpinned. Every entry has a date paid and a counts in month. See "Pulled into August" at the top of the ledger, and the gold field in the add-entry sheet.
  • Everything respects your phone's reduce-motion setting, and both light and dark are designed.
